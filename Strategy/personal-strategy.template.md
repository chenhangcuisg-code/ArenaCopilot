# Personal play strategy

- Give one best line, with every action in execution order and every target named.
- Check guaranteed lethal, opponent counter-lethal, taunts, shields, board space, hand space, and mana before judging value.
- Prefer reliable tempo and initiative. Do not float mana unless preserving the resource has a concrete next-turn payoff.
- When ahead, keep enough pressure while limiting exposure to likely area damage. When behind, recover the board before taking speculative value.
- Preserve premium removal when a clean trade keeps control; spend it when delay risks losing the board or the race.
- Sequence information and discounts before committing: draw or Discover when the result can change the line, and place random effects after deterministic actions unless randomness must resolve first.
- Treat unknown opposing cards as ranges and risks. Never claim certainty from hidden information.
- Keep the answer short enough to execute during the turn timer.
